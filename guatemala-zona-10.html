Cadejo Guatemala, Zona Viva, Zona 10

The craft beer spot in the heart of Zona Viva, Zona 10. A green, relaxed space to get away from the routine: wait out the traffic or meet up with your friends, your partner, your dog, your mother-in-law or the office crew. Our open-air garden and terraces are waiting.

Our beers10

Pick a size: 4 oz Q13, 12 oz Q35, 16 oz Q45, 23 oz Q60.

  • Roja (Red Ale)

    Red as the Cadejo's own eyes: sweet, spiced, with a bitterness that will make your fangs sweat.

  • WAPA (Wheat American Pale Ale)

    The WAPA is a quiet killer. Its blonde look hides an intense citrus bitterness that charms the most seasoned palates.

  • La Negra (Irish Stout)

    Creamy and dark with coffee and chocolate notes. They say it is the blood of the Black Cadejo.

  • Mera Belga (Belgian White Ale)

    A unique style with Belgian yeast, orange and spices. EVERYONE likes the Belga for its smooth, fruity notes. It stands for the kindness of the White Cadejo.

  • Hija de Pooh (Honey Blonde Ale)

    A famous little bear left his daughter to be raised by the Cadejos. She loves natural honey and beer, so we combined them to please her.

  • Suegra (India Pale Ale)

    Like our mothers in law, this beer is strong, sweet and bitter, yet a curious character that gives us a glimpse of their children's future. How could you not love them?

  • Koloscha (Kölsch German Ale)

    A beer loved by the pack, inspired by classic lagers but with soft, delicious fruity notes that will curl your eyelashes.

  • La Nacional

    Our lager: fresh flavor, hop aroma and strong malt character. The best way to start drinking craft beer.

  • Seasonal beers

    Cadejo is quality and variety. Every month our brewing team creates a new style just for our restaurants. Seasonal beer prices vary, ask your server.
