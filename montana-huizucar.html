Locations / Destinations

Cadejo Montaña, Calle a Huizúcar, Nuevo Cuscatlán

Our only steakhouse: meats cooked over charcoal and wood, paired with our craft beers, with panoramic views of the country's most iconic volcanoes and lakes. The mountain without leaving the city, just 5 minutes away.

Your escape from the city

Surrounded by nature and 3 minutes from PriceSmart Santa Elena. Made for family weekends, with breakfast from 7 a.m. on Saturday and Sunday, for celebrations and for company events. Some nights we host comedy in our VIP room.
