Cadejo Playa, El Sunzal, Surf City

Our restaurant in Surf City, on the cliffs of El Sunzal. Seafood and international dishes for the whole family, our craft beer with views of the Pacific, a seawater pool, a freshwater pool and rooms to wake up facing the beach.

Pools and rooms

To use the seawater and freshwater pools there is a $25 minimum per person, and all of it goes toward your food and drinks. Coming just to eat? No minimum.

Stay the night: rooms start at $125 and you wake up facing the beach.

On weekends we open at 9 a.m. for breakfast.
