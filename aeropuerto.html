Cadejo Aeropuerto, Airport, Gates 2 and 9

Two restaurants inside El Salvador International Airport, in front of Gate 2 and Gate 9. Good food and our craft beers, with breakfast, lunch and dinner. A relaxed stop while you wait for your flight, and your last Salvadoran beer before you go.
