Why Cadejo?

In Salvadoran legend, the cadejo is the dog-like spirit that walks beside people at night and looks after them on the way home. It's the name we chose for El Salvador's first craft brewery.

Questions people ask

Is Cadejo a brewery or a restaurant?

Both. Cadejo started in 2012 as El Salvador's first craft brewery. Today every location is a restaurant with its own kitchen, and the newer ones are built for families, celebrations and long meals.

Are all Cadejo locations the same?

No. Playa, Mansión and Montaña are destinations for families, couples and visitors. Zona Rosa is the brewery. Santa Elena, Soyapango, Opico and Guatemala are neighborhood spots with music, games and sports. Each location has its own menu.

Can I bring my kids?

Yes. Every Cadejo location is family friendly and kids are welcome. Montaña, Mansión, Playa and Soyapango also serve breakfast on weekends.

How do the pools at Cadejo Playa work?

To use the saltwater and freshwater pools, there's a $25 minimum per person, and all of it goes toward your food and drinks. Coming just to eat? No minimum.

Can I stay overnight at Cadejo Playa?

Yes. Cadejo Playa in El Sunzal has rooms from $125 per night.

Can I visit the brewery?

Yes. There's a brewery tour every Thursday at 7 p.m. at Cadejo Zona Rosa, where all our beer is made.

Where can I watch the game?

Santa Elena has the most screens. Zona Rosa, Soyapango and Opico also show the big matches.
